## Our story

The idea came during founder **Shatha Hajj Hayya**’s first pregnancy with her son **Nadeem**. She looked for a special **Arabic** book to capture the sweet, important moments of early motherhood—and when she couldn’t find one, she designed it herself. That became **PEEK STORE**, now available for your child too.

This book is dedicated to her children, Nadeem and Ward—the reason the project exists.
